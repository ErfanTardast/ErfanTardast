### 🚀 Featured work

#### 🌾 KEYVAN — premium rice brand storefront
Persian-first production storefront for the **KEYVAN / کیوان** rice brand. Editorial landing page, catalogue, product-selection and comparison tools, wholesale and brand pages, and a WhatsApp order-request flow designed around how the business actually sells. The design direction was picked from four competing prototypes built in a dedicated design lab.
<br/>`Next.js 16` `React 19` `TypeScript` `Tailwind CSS 4` `GSAP` `Motion` `Playwright`

#### 📈 TradeMaster AI — discipline OS for traders
Bilingual Persian/English workspace that helps traders plan every trade, journal quickly, review their behaviour, catch repeated mistakes and build discipline across Forex, crypto and global stocks.
<br/>`Next.js 16` `TypeScript` `Prisma` `PostgreSQL` `Zod` `Recharts` `Docker`

#### 🧪 Trading Lab — systematic trading research platform
Modular crypto + forex research platform built for honest out-of-sample testing. Pipeline: causal forecasts → forecast combiner → volatility-targeted portfolio → covariance overlay → risk engine → backtest / paper trading.
<br/>`Python` `FastAPI` `pandas` `NumPy` `scikit-learn` `Optuna` `pytest` `Docker`

#### 🛡️ RiskManagerEA — MetaTrader 5 risk panel
Manual MT5 order panel with safety built in: tick-aware position sizing from account equity, broker stop/freeze validation, multi-TP allocation, and separate execution paths for hedging and netting accounts. Covered by deterministic tests.
<br/>`MQL5` `MetaTrader 5`

#### 🍚 Darya Rice — luxury e-commerce concept
Persian RTL storefront with cart, search, quick-view, filters, editorial storytelling and a custom motion system.
<br/>`Next.js 14` `TypeScript` `Zustand` `GitHub Actions`

> Most of this work is private client or product code. Demos and walkthroughs are available on request.
